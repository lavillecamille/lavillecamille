      - **Mali and Niger, 2025–2026. FCDO (CLEAN Helpdesk), for the World Bank.** Lead author of the case study of the JASS programme (GBP 19 million, 24 communes), which fed the World Bank report on the peace and social dividends of climate action; mixed methods, including an ACLED event-density analysis around programme sites.
      - **Syria, 2026. ODI Global.** National and governorate-level population scenarios to 2045, used in water and electricity demand modelling.
      - **Yemen, 2025–2026. UK Government Yemen Office Network (CLEAN Helpdesk).** Design of the systematic review, interview questionnaires and gender and social inclusion approach of a climate, environment and conflict risk diagnostic.
      - **Chad, 2025. FCDO (SPARC), with the Chadian NGO ADES.** Study lead on refugee women's access to land in three refugee-hosting sites in eastern Chad (30 focus groups, factorial vignettes, training of partner teams).
      - **Eastern DRC, 2025. FCDO (SPARC).** Study lead on local conflict dynamics, environmental degradation and climate hazards in North Kivu, South Kivu and Ituri, including spatial analysis of settlements, deforestation and hazard exposure.
      - **Papua New Guinea, 2024–2025. USAID and FCDO.** Study lead on locally led climate and security action, including a two-day participatory workshop in Port Moresby. Country lead for Papua New Guinea in the UNFCCC FINCOAHN project, with the Climate Change and Development Authority.
      - **Chad, 2024. FCDO (SPARC).** Lead author of Chad's climate-finance readiness assessment, in support of the FCDO-led working group on access to climate finance.
      - **Sahel, 2023. World Bank (Sahel Adaptive Social Protection Program).** Systematic review of the effects of social safety nets in fragile, conflict and violence-affected contexts.
      - **2017. AFD.** Analytical framework for socio-political risk in country-risk analysis.

      Open to research collaborations and selected advisory work, in French and English: laville.ecodef[at]gmail.com
