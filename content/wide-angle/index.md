    title: "Wide angle: shocks, arms and finance"
    subtitle: ""
    text: |
      Zooming out from the regions, three questions run through my work: how transboundary economic, climate and political shocks translate into local conflict risk; how states finance and equip their armed forces; and how climate finance reaches, or fails to reach, fragile states.

      - **Shocks.** A meta-analysis of the empirical literature on shocks and internal conflict (*Journal of Peace Research*, 2025), with [replication files](https://github.com/lavillecamille/Replication-files).
      - **Arms.** The political economy of military aircraft in Europe (*Defence and Peace Economics*, 2024); military spending and development aid in the Sahel (Ferdi, 2016).
      - **Risk and finance.** Socio-political risk in country-risk analysis (AFD, 2018), applied by AFD economists to Turkey, Tunisia and Lebanon; a pathway to climate-resilient development in fragile settings (ODI Global, 2023), cited by ILO (2025) and CREWS (2024); work in progress on climate risk sharing in World Bank projects and on gender in Japan's climate finance.
